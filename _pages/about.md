**Hi there, I'm Wanqing Zhang** 👋


I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in Engineering Science and Mechanics at [The Pennsylvania State University](https://www.psu.edu/), working under the supervision of [Prof. Huanyu Cheng](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=YKzDLy0AAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao).  My current research focuses on developing functional materials and sensors for health monitoring and human-machine interaction, with a particular emphasis on improving the reliability of bioelectronic interfaces.  My recent work includes paintable on-skin electrodes, hydrogel-based biosensors, and conductive polymer bioelectronic interfaces for long-term physiological monitoring.


Beyond research, I am passionate about translating laboratory innovations into real-world healthcare technologies.  I have participated in the Ben Franklin TechCelerator and the NSF Mid-Atlantic I-Corps program and will continue this effort through the NSF National I-Corps program.  By integrating advances in materials science, electronics, and biomedical engineering, I aim to develop practical bioelectronic technologies for personalized healthcare.


Prior to joining Penn State in 2021, I earned dual Bachelor of Engineering degrees in Electronics and Electrical Engineering through a joint program between [the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)](https://en.uestc.edu.cn/) and [the University of Glasgow](https://www.gla.ac.uk/), graduating with First-Class Honours.
